Start with the census

Choose the census you upload on the census page (Excel .xlsx or CSV, any layout). Why first: it answers most of the questions below for you (ages, where employees live, how many are eligible, who enrolls), so fewer are left to answer, and the enrollment comes from the census instead of an assumption. It is read here, in your browser, and never sent anywhere: only counts are kept. You can change any answer it fills.

No census in this layout yet? Download the census template (Excel): one row per eligible employee, with the columns these questions need. Fill it in, then choose it here.
